What2Craft

A tool for finding what to craft in FFXIV!
  • A rough reference for what crafts might be profitable
  • Could not exist without Universalis! All data on this site was collected using the Universalis API.
  • In general, the list estimates the profit margins for all sellable, craftable items
  • The list assumes all ingredients for the craft are bought on the market board
  • Sort by daily potential to find profitable items that also sell plenty!
  • By default, the list only shows items that sell at least 0.5 times per day
  • Hover over est. materials to see the ingredient list and the prices of each ingredient!
  • Prices shown can be assumed to be for NQ if NQ items are more common, and HQ if HQ items are more common. If they are equally common, prices consider both.
